# Use of Artificial Intelligence

Agni's codebase is built with **Claude Code** (Anthropic). This is a short running log of how AI was used, for the Space Apps project page.

All scientific choices (harmonization method, validation design, thresholds) are reviewed by the team, and every number shown in the app comes from NASA FIRMS data processed by our pipeline. Nothing is generated by an AI at runtime.
